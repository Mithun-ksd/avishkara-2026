About the exhibition

Nine projects, one showcase

Avishkara Sharada 2026 is a National-Level PU College Fest organized by Sharada Degree College, bringing together young minds from different institutions to celebrate creativity, talent, innovation, and teamwork. As part of this prestigious event, BCA students presented 9 innovative projects across multiple categories, showcasing their technical skills, creative thinking, problem-solving abilities, and passion for technology. The projects reflect the students’ dedication to innovation and their commitment to transforming ideas into practical solutions.
